Finger Lakes Anthro Weekend

May 28 – 31, 2026 • Camp Whitman, NY

F.L.A.W. is an outdoor furry fandom weekend with exclusive access to Camp Whitman in Penn Yan, NY. Participants stay in cabins, are fed 6 delicious meals, and can enjoy activities including canoeing in Seneca Lake, fishing, games, campfires, crafting, and more. We hope to provide a fun weekend to relax, enjoy nature, and make memories together!

We are a volunteer-run, not-for-profit organization officially founded in 2026. With every event we raise money for charity and hope you can join us in raising funds to help good causes.
